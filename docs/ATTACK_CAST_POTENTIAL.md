# Attack and cast potential

The character-sheet badges show a maximum for one of the character's turns, not what remains in the current turn.

- `Attacks A + B`: `A` is the number of attacks in the primary Attack action. `B` is the best compatible combination outside those normal attacks.
- `Casts A + B`: `A` is the primary 2014 Cast a Spell action or 2024 Magic action cast. `B` is the best compatible combination of Bonus Action or additional-action casts.
- Full resources and valid owned equipment are assumed. A spell such as Haste may already be active, but the detail tree marks that setup.
- Reactions, save-only effects, summoned creatures, and attacks made by controlled creatures are excluded.
- Variable multi-target actions stay out of the numeric headline and appear under “Depends on targets.”

## Audited fifth-edition rules

The built-in calculations follow the official CC-licensed [SRD 5.1](https://media.wizards.com/2023/downloads/dnd/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf) and [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf):

- Extra Attack for Fighter, Barbarian, Monk, Paladin, and Ranger; multiclass Extra Attack features do not add together.
- Thirsting Blade and Devouring Blade when their invocation features are actually present.
- Action Surge, including the 2024 restriction that its added action cannot be a Magic action.
- Martial Arts and Flurry of Blows, including the 2024 level-10 third Flurry strike.
- Two-weapon/Light attacks, plus the 2024 Nick and Cleave mastery interactions when the character can use Weapon Mastery.
- Berserker Frenzy, Hunter sources when the corresponding selected feature is present, Haste, Scimitar of Speed, and Dancing Sword.
- Quickened Spell, Bonus Action spell conflicts, the 2014 action-cantrip restriction, and the 2024 one-spell-slot-per-turn restriction.
- Multi-roll attack spells such as Eldritch Blast and Scorching Ray.

## Private content

Commercial/non-SRD names such as Polearm Master are not shipped as built-in rules. A private feat, feature, spell, weapon, or item can attach reviewed structured contributions: count, timing, resource cost, setup, hit/target conditions, and required weapon properties. Descriptive prose is never parsed for automation.
